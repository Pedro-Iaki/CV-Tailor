Senior Data Engineer - Acme Pagamentos

We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to build and operate our lakehouse on
Databricks and Azure. You will design PySpark pipelines, model data in Delta Lake,
orchestrate ingestion with Azure Data Factory, and improve CI/CD for data jobs.

Requirements:
- 4+ years with Spark (PySpark / Spark SQL) and Delta Lake
- Azure (ADF, ADLS), Databricks
- Python, SQL, testing with pytest
- Experience with dbt and Kafka is a plus
